# Nexpla Theme Reference

Use this as the visual baseline for future Nexpla portfolio/platform page work.

## Overall Feel

- Premium, calm, investor-facing SaaS aesthetic.
- Airy white page sections with generous vertical spacing.
- Dark forest/teal intelligence panels used as emphasis moments.
- Editorial typography: large high-contrast serif headlines paired with clean sans-serif labels and body copy.
- Minimal decoration, mostly expressed through grids, soft borders, muted glow, and structured cards.

## Color Language

- Primary dark: near-black green/forest for panels and footer.
- Primary action: deep teal buttons.
- Accent: bright aqua/teal signal dots, labels, and glows.
- Body text: muted sage/gray-green.
- Surfaces: white or very pale mint with thin teal-tinted borders.
- Avoid loud gradients or decorative blobs; use subtle radial glows and grid lines instead.

## Layout Patterns

- Sticky/light top navigation with logo left, centered pill nav, and CTAs right.
- First viewport should feel spacious, with a large serif headline and a dark product/intelligence panel.
- Section headers often pair a large serif title on the left with muted explanatory text on the right.
- Cards are large, quiet, lightly bordered, and softly shadowed.
- Dark feature sections use grid overlays and rounded corners.
- Footer uses the dark grid theme: white logo, short positioning line, status pill, nav tiles, investor access card, and bottom chips.

## UI Details

- Buttons: deep teal filled primary, outlined teal secondary.
- Navigation: rounded segmented pill, active item on white.
- Cards: subtle border, 8-14px radius depending on scale, restrained shadow.
- Labels: uppercase, spaced lettering, teal.
- Floating Ask Nexpla button: dark pill with icon and white text, bottom-right.

## Content Tone

- Direct, confident, concise.
- Phrases should feel specific to pharma ERP, workflow intelligence, AI-native rebuilds, and investor/operator trust.
- Avoid generic marketing filler.
