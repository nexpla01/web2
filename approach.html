Our approach

Modernize. Rebuild. Repeat.

We partner with profitable legacy pharma ERP businesses and run a single repeatable playbook — modernizing their operations, then rebuilding their platform on AI-native architecture. One playbook. Defensible. Built for compounding.

1

Partner with established ERP businesses

We identify profitable pharma ERP businesses that have built something valuable over decades — customer relationships, domain knowledge, transaction data, market trust. The software is old. Everything around it is not.

  • Existing customers who already depend on the platform
  • Proven, recurring revenue from a sticky customer base
  • Deep pharma workflow knowledge embedded in 300+ modules
  • 20+ years of transactional data with real business intelligence value
2

Modernize operations and customer experience

Before rebuilding the core, we stabilize and improve. This creates immediate value for customers and funds the longer rebuild — while making the data and workflows legible for AI.

  • Streamline support and internal operations for efficiency
  • Improve reliability, onboarding, and customer UX
  • Data migration and cleansing to prepare for the intelligence layer
  • Unlock immediate retention and upsell opportunities
3

Rebuild on modern, AI-native architecture

With operations stable and data accessible, we rebuild the core platform — not from a blank screen, but grounded in decades of real pharma workflows and business logic. AI agents do in months what once took years.

  • Rebuild core modules on modern, cloud-native infrastructure
  • AI-native interface: natural language, voice, intelligent recommendations
  • Agentic capabilities: the system can understand, decide, and execute
  • APIs and services that others can build on top of
4

Repeat with the next ERP

Each rebuild compounds. The platform becomes richer with each new ERP partner — more data, more workflows, more ecosystem integrations, more intelligence. The playbook scales without reinventing itself.

  • Shared intelligence layer grows more capable with each addition
  • Ecosystem connections (fintech, logistics, compliance) multiply in value
  • Data network effects: better recommendations with more context
  • One team, one playbook — higher margins with each iteration

One playbook. Built to compound.

The repeating cycle isn't just a strategy — it's a flywheel. Every ERP partnership adds more context, more data, more customers, and deeper intelligence to the platform. The value compounds.

Higher growth

Modern products reach more customers than legacy software ever could.

Higher margins

AI-native operations cost dramatically less to run than manual-heavy legacy support.

Stronger retention

A better product means customers don't leave. A modern ERP is a stickier ERP.

Compounding value

Reinvest, repeat. Each cycle makes the platform more valuable for the next.
