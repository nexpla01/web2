What we look for

What makes an ERP
the right partnership candidate.

We're selective. The context accumulated around the software matters more than the software itself.

Customers who depend on it

We look for sticky, long-tenure customer relationships. If customers have stayed for 10+ years, they're not leaving for a new entrant — but they will upgrade for a better product they already trust.

Rich, longitudinal data

The more years of transaction history, the better the AI intelligence we can build. We prefer ERPs with 15+ years of structured data across inventory, billing, and distribution.

Profitable, not broken

We are not turnaround investors. We look for businesses with proven recurring revenue — ideally AMC or subscription-based — where the fundamentals are sound and the problem is the technology.

Deep pharma workflow specificity

Generic ERPs won't do. We want platforms built specifically for pharma — with expiry management, schedule-H compliance, distribution-level billing, and the other nuances that generalist software gets wrong.

Founder or family-owned

These businesses were built by people who understand pharma. That domain knowledge is embedded in the software. We want to partner with those operators, not strip mine what they built.

Ready for the next chapter

Succession, fatigue with competing on old software, or a genuine desire to see their product modernized. We work best with owners who want to see their platform become what it could have been.
