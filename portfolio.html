Acquisition model

What makes an ERP worth acquiring.

We acquire the context to rebuild: customers, data, workflows, and domain knowledge. The right asset already sits between critical transactions in the supply chain.

01

Customers who depend on it

Sticky, long-tenure relationships signal software that already sits inside daily business operations and can upgrade without forcing customers to switch vendors.

02

Rich, longitudinal data

Years of structured inventory, billing, collection, and distribution data become the training ground for decision support and agentic workflows.

03

Profitable, not broken

We are not turnaround investors. We prefer recurring revenue, durable customer trust, and a technology ceiling that Nexpla can lift.

04

Deep pharma workflow specificity

Expiry management, schedule-H compliance, distributor billing, stockist behavior, and local operating nuance matter more than generic ERP breadth.

05

Founder or family-owned

The best assets were built by operators who understand pharma. We preserve that embedded knowledge and convert it into a modern product system.

06

Ready for the next chapter

Succession, ownership transition, or ambition to scale can all create the right moment for a confidential next chapter.
